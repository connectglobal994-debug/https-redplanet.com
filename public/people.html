TEAM OPERATING SYSTEMS

One team. Individual operating systems.

Red Planet currently has around 10 team members. Each person should have a clear role-based workspace—a practical operating system for their day-to-day work, not a separate computer operating system.

PERSONAL WORKSPACE

My work

Each team member sees their responsibilities, priorities, tasks, deadlines, handovers and current progress in one place.

ROLE CLARITY

My responsibilities

A role description, decision boundaries, manager, collaborators and expected deliverables make ownership clear.

PERFORMANCE

My goals & KPIs

Role-specific targets, evidence of completed work, check-ins, feedback and development actions.

TEAM COORDINATION

Shared operations

Team-wide projects, dependencies, announcements, meetings, shared resources and escalation paths.

How each person's workspace should work

1. Assign a person and role

Create an individual account; assign a role, manager, permissions and team.

2. Set the operating plan

Agree responsibilities, weekly priorities, deliverables, deadlines and role-specific KPIs.

3. Work and report

Team members update task status, attach work evidence, flag blockers and request decisions.

4. Review and improve

Managers review progress, quality, workload and support needs on a regular schedule.

Important: Each person gets an individual account and tailored workspace—not a shared login or shared password. Access to finance, candidate records, customer data, trading and administrator controls is restricted by role. Managers see only the team information they are authorized to manage.

RED PLANET AI TRAINING PORTAL

LEARN. PRACTISE. BUILD.

A dedicated training portal where every team member can learn with Red Planet AI while continuing to deliver work. Training is organised around the company's mission and each person's role.

LEARN

Role-based learning paths

Guided lessons for AI tools, company systems, workflows, security, product knowledge and role-specific skills.

PRACTISE

AI training workspace

A safe sandbox to ask questions, practise tasks, test ideas and receive coaching without changing live systems.

MEASURE

Progress and assessments

Quizzes, practical exercises, learning milestones and completion records for each team member.

IMPROVE

Team knowledge base

Approved Red Planet guides, policies and project documentation that the AI can use to give consistent answers.

Training versus model training: Team members can train and improve their skills by working with the AI. Their chats do not automatically retrain the underlying AI model. Any use of feedback or work materials to improve shared knowledge must be permission-based, reviewed and access-controlled.

Proposed hiring workflow

1. Define the need

Approve the business case, budget, role description and success measures.

2. Attract and screen

Publish the opportunity, assess relevant experience and shortlist against transparent criteria.

3. Interview and assess

Run structured interviews and role-relevant practical assessments; record decisions consistently.

4. Approve and onboard

Confirm references where appropriate, written terms, access permissions, responsibilities and onboarding plan.

5. Review performance

Set baseline KPIs, hold regular check-ins and document coaching, blockers and outcomes.

SAMPLE KPI

Delivery

Milestones completed on time; work documented; blockers raised early.

SAMPLE KPI

Quality

Acceptance-test pass rate, defect rate, security and accessibility checks.

SAMPLE KPI

Business impact

Adoption, customer outcomes, operational savings or revenue contribution where relevant.

SAMPLE KPI

Collaboration

Communication, handover quality, reliability and contribution to team goals.
